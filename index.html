    1. 9.1 When Ax = b has no solution

    5. 9.5 The Gram–Schmidt process and the QR factorization
    6. 9.6 Least-squares solutions
  10. Lecture 10: The Spectral Theorem and the Singular Value Decomposition

    Symmetric matrices and the spectral theorem, quadratic forms, principal axes and definiteness, maximum and minimum of a quadratic form on the unit sphere, the singular value decomposition and what it tells about a matrix, the pseudoinverse and the shortest least-squares solution, and best approximation by matrices of low rank (Eckart–Young), with image compression and principal components.

    1. 10.1 Symmetric matrices, the spectral theorem, and quadratic forms

    4. 10.4 Best approximation by matrices of low rank
